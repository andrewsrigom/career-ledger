# Compose career material from the ledger

Use `$career-compose-material` to create `<resume | portfolio narrative | professional summary | interview story | evidence-gap report>` for `<audience or target role>`.

Use the existing private activities and workstreams as the source of truth. Preserve attribution, evidence strength, uncertainty, and privacy. Select and compress real evidence; do not invent new experience, impact, metrics, ownership, or production status.

Keep the result private. If any part should later become public, send it through sanitization, publication review, and explicit owner approval as a separate step.
