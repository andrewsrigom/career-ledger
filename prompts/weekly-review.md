# Weekly career review

Review all scans created since the last weekly review.

For each configured project:

1. use `$career-analyze-project` when new evidence supports meaningful activity or progression;
2. use `$career-consolidate-work` to update ongoing workstreams and remove duplication without erasing chronology;
3. use `$career-review-outcomes` for mature workstreams;
4. identify reached milestones, evidence-backed achievements, and workstreams that may be ready for sanitization;
5. do not publish or approve anything.

Finish with a concise private summary of new activities, workstream progression, milestones, achievements, high-value questions, and candidates worth preparing.
