Review all scans created since the last weekly review.

1. use `$career-analyze-project` when new evidence exists;
2. use `$career-consolidate-work` to update ongoing initiatives and remove duplication;
3. use `$career-review-outcomes` for mature initiatives;
4. list initiatives that may be ready for sanitization;
5. do not publish or approve anything.

Finish with a concise private summary of new initiatives, updated initiatives, questions for me, and candidates worth preparing.
