# Compose Career Material

## Goal

Project the strongest relevant evidence from the ledger into a specific career artifact without changing the underlying facts, attribution, confidence, or privacy status.

## Sources

Read the relevant private activities and workstreams first. Use approved public records when the requested output must contain only already-public information. Legacy initiatives may provide context, but prefer the first-class chronological records when available.

Never create experience independently of the ledger. Missing evidence is an enrichment opportunity, not permission to complete a persuasive sentence speculatively.

## Workflow

1. Identify the artifact, audience, and purpose. For a targeted resume, identify the role's strongest required signals.
2. Search the ledger for supported evidence and rank it by relevance, impact, evidence strength, ownership, scale, recency, and distinctiveness.
3. Select complementary examples instead of repeating the same capability.
4. Preserve dates, ownership verbs, provenance, uncertainty, and the separation between deliverables and outcomes.
5. Write with high information density. Use technology names when they explain complexity or establish relevant expertise, not as an achievement substitute.
6. Keep the result private unless it is separately sanitized, reviewed, and owner-approved through the publication workflow.

## Projection shapes

- **Timeline:** chronological activities, progression, milestones, and achievements.
- **Project page:** context, responsibility, decisions, workstreams, milestones, outcomes, and technologies.
- **Portfolio:** selected evidence that reveals sustained professional themes.
- **Resume:** concise evidence selected for one target role; tailoring changes selection and emphasis, not facts.
- **Interview story:** Context, Problem, Constraints, Decision, Action, Result, Tradeoffs, and Learning.
- **Evidence-gap report:** specific missing facts that would materially affect impact, ownership, scale, public safety, accuracy, or relevance.

## Output

Write drafts under `.career/private/projections/<artifact-type>/` unless the user requests an in-chat response. Do not modify `content/public/`, candidates, or approval metadata.

Finish by listing the ledger records used, claims intentionally omitted, and any high-value enrichment questions.
